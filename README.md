# Amazon Electronics Sales Analysis

A data-analysis portfolio project focused on exploring electronics product and sales-related data to identify patterns that can inform commercial decisions.

## Project Objective

The goal is to turn a raw product dataset into a clear analytical story: understand what the data contains, validate its quality, investigate useful comparisons, and communicate findings in business terms.

## Questions to Explore

Depending on the fields available in the dataset, useful questions may include:

- Which product categories or brands have the highest representation?
- How do prices and ratings vary across products or categories?
- Is there a relationship between price, rating, and review volume?
- Which products appear to offer strong value under clearly defined criteria?
- What data-quality issues could affect the conclusions?

These are analysis questions, **not findings**. Conclusions should be added only after running the analysis and validating the outputs.

## Recommended Workflow

1. **Inspect the dataset:** identify the source, dimensions, columns, data types, and unit of analysis.
2. **Clean the data:** check missing values, duplicates, inconsistent labels, currency formats, and invalid records.
3. **Explore:** calculate descriptive statistics and compare meaningful groups.
4. **Visualize:** choose charts that directly answer the analytical questions.
5. **Communicate:** summarize verified findings, limitations, and practical next steps.
